About this search engine

A full-text search engine built from scratch over Philippine Supreme Court decisions (2010-2026). No Elasticsearch, no Lucene, no Postgres full-text search, no vector database, no embedding API anywhere in the retrieval path.

Every component (crawler, parser, tokenizer, inverted index, and ranking function) is hand-written in Python.

Query syntax

  • Terms - grave abuse of discretion (all terms must appear)
  • OR - mandamus OR certiorari
  • Phrases - "beyond reasonable doubt"
  • Citation - G.R. No. 192393 or gr:192393
  • Ponente - ponente:Leonen
  • Year - year:2019
  • Division - division:"En Banc"
  • Combined - ponente:Caguioa year:2019 certiorari
